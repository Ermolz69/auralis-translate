## Admission sequence

1. **Freeze provenance outside the code tree.** Record the exact media and SRT page revisions, retrieval timestamp, raw SRT SHA-256, caption contributors, source license and attribution, reference license, and permitted uses in a versioned corpus manifest. The Chinese candidate has a pinned raw copy and hash, but no approved rights decision or admitted manifest. The Japanese SRT revision remains unknown in this inventory. If rights cannot be established, leave the item as metadata only.
2. **Confirm that the input is the product's input.** A bilingual reviewer checks whether the source-language caption represents the audible speech, whether the dialogue has missing or mistranscribed spans, and whether the language/script label is accurate. The strict parser must accept the exact raw bytes before inference. Record rejected cues or files explicitly; do not silently repair the source.
3. **Create a source-grid reference.** A Chinese/Russian or Japanese/Russian reviewer writes or verifies Russian lines from the source captions and scene context while retaining every eligible source cue ID and timing. A second reviewer adjudicates ambiguous dialect terms, speaker changes, and critical meaning disputes. Other-language tracks are context, not automatic gold.
4. **Use pilots for development first.** Both examples can exercise acquisition, parsing, reviewer workflow and model comparison on development scenes after admission. Assign entire media items to one split and keep them out of a frozen release holdout once they have influenced model, prompt, or glossary selection.
5. **Expand coverage before a gate.** Seek independently licensed native-speech scenes with other speakers and topics, standard Mandarin and Japanese alongside dialects, traditional Chinese if advertised, and varied cue lengths. Target the [evaluation protocol's](001-open-data-and-language-gates.md#frozen-split-and-coverage) roughly 20–30 scenes and 500 cues per language, separated by media into development and untouched holdout. A reviewer must score that holdout before S8 or S9 can pass.

Record the disposition of each candidate as `discovered`, `rights_checked`, `source_checked`, `reference_reviewed`, `development_only`, `holdout_frozen`, or `rejected`, with the evidence and reason for every transition. These are **corpus curation states**, not product run states in Translate SQLite.
