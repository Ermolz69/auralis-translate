This guard has **not yet** passed a new complete natural model run.

The two runs total 94 chats and 288 HTTP requests on the same source, but they are an initial attempt and one stochastic recovery, not a 1.8B/7B quality comparison. Next work: test the format guard through a fresh bounded real candidate, inspect cue transitions and semantic facts before any speech adaptation, and assemble/listen to a natural audio pilot only from an explicitly reviewed script.
