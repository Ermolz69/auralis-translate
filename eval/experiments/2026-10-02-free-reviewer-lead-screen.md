# No-budget Chinese–Russian reviewer lead screen

Date: 2 October 2026. Scope: identify a voluntary, opt-in route for a small
**development** review packet, without messaging anyone or reclassifying a
person as a reviewer. This does not admit a source, fill the 300-cue sealed
holdout, or satisfy G3/G4/A4. Public profiles can be stale and self-reported
skills must be checked before any judgments count.

## One relevant invitation lead

The public [LiYuZi volunteer localization post on itch.io](https://itch.io/t/6087896/lfpfree-localizationruenperhaps-zh-translator-looking-to-join-indie-projects)
was created on 11 March 2026 in its Help Wanted or Offered forum. The author
self-reports native Russian, a degree in English and Chinese linguistics,
translation experience, and willingness to join an indie project without pay.
The same post names **English/Russian as the main working pair** and describes
Chinese only as a spoken language and a possible localization direction.
Chinese→Russian review proficiency, availability in October, permission to
review a commercial product, identity and independence are **unverified**.
The post invites contact, but no message or source packet has been sent.

This is more relevant to a bounded invitation than a generic language-exchange
listing because voluntary localization is explicitly offered. The first ask
must be only a 15–20-minute, four-cue written Chinese/Russian development
screen from the rights-cleared Paywall packet. Explain that its film speech
is English, the project is commercial, the exercise is unpaid, and no further
work is implied. Do not send private restaurant audio, full subtitle files,
sealed holdout content, model identities or reference answers. Ask whether
Chinese→Russian source evaluation is within the volunteer's actual skill and
whether they consent to private retention and pseudonymized aggregate reporting.
Only after an affirmative response should the private four-cue packet be sent.
The existing [consent and review packet](2026-10-02-paywall-volunteer-review-packet.md)
contains the blinded procedure and source attribution.

Draft first invitation, **not sent**:

> Hello LiYu, I saw your volunteer localization post. We are building a
> commercial Chinese-to-Russian subtitle tool and are looking for one small,
> unpaid evaluation of four written Chinese subtitle cues and two blind Russian
> candidates per cue (about 15–20 minutes). The subtitles are CC BY 4.0; the
> film itself is spoken in English, so this tests written Chinese only. Would
> Chinese→Russian source-meaning review be within your skills, and would you
> be interested in this limited exercise? We would keep your detailed answers
> private and publish only pseudonymized aggregate error counts with your
> consent. There is no obligation to do any further work. I can explain the
> source, rubric, retention and withdrawal terms before sending any material.

## Channels screened and exclusions

- [HelloTalk](https://www.hellotalk.com/en/learn/russian) and
  [Tandem](https://tandem.net/) advertise free language exchange and correction
  tools. Neither provides a verified Chinese→Russian reviewer or consent for
  commercial quality assurance. Use only for a genuinely reciprocal small
  exchange, if the owner can provide one.
- The current [r/language_exchange welcome/rules](https://www.reddit.com/r/language_exchange/comments/1q6q8o1/read_this_first_welcome_to_rlanguage_exchange/)
  require genuine language exchange and prohibit advertising. A request for
  free commercial review there would misrepresent the task, so this channel
  is not selected for this invitation.
- An existing [Russian/Chinese exchange post](https://www.reddit.com/r/language_exchange/comments/1tx59mp/offering_russian_seeking_english_chinese/)
  mentions a self-reported HSK6, but its author seeks reciprocal conversation
  and expresses personal partner preferences. It is not a reviewer offer and
  is not selected for commercial project outreach.

The first contact needs explicit owner authorization because
[`docs/GOAL_PROMPT.md`](../../docs/GOAL_PROMPT.md) bars sending messages to
others without it. If authorized, send **one** tailored invitation through the
public post's contact route, record the date and exact text privately, and stop
if the person declines or does not opt in. Do not interpret silence as consent.
An independent reviewer and the second critical-error adjudicator remain
unavailable until actual affirmative, qualified participation is recorded.

## Owner decision after the screen

The owner declined the proposed volunteer invitation on 2 October 2026.
No contact will be made and this lead is retained only as historical research.
The no-budget human-acquisition path is inactive under the current preference.
Automated/AI checks and Russian audio engineering can continue, but they do
not supply independent bilingual or listening judgments. The full G3/G4/A4
and RELEASE-05 gates remain open until the owner identifies an acceptable
human-review route or explicitly changes the delivery scope.
