# Chip/core warning v1: offline exposed-source screen

Date: 10 October 2026. Tasks: `EVAL-04`, `CTX-03`, `LONG-04`.
Two frozen prompt changes failed to preserve the exact denied referent in
REG-077/079. This experiment measures a conservative *warning*, not a
rewrite or product-quality claim. The source rule triggers only on an
explicit denial of `多核` or one multi-core chip, excludes processor-count
mentions and quoted metalinguistic examples, and warns only if the Russian
target contains `многопроцессор...`. A separate target-language warning
recognizes the ungrammatical `один ядро`. Neither rule changes output,
checkpoint or approval state. A missing warning is not language acceptance.

Freeze the rule bytes, the REG-077 v4 machine/AI reports, and existing
private SRT identities before screening. Use all 192 v4 replies with their
source-aware AI labels, plus complete 467-cue Vivo/MediaTek drafts from
7B/v8 and 1.8B/v8, the 268-cue ASUS 7B/v8 draft and both 263-cue Sethlui
restaurant v6 drafts. The five complete drafts contain 1,728 aligned
source/target pairs in three correlated source groups; with the v4 cases,
the total is 1,920 paired observations. All material is exposed
development data, not a sealed holdout. Compare warning hits against
the pinned AI labels in the v4 set only; report full-draft trigger counts,
warning IDs and unverifiable precision separately. Do not treat two
drafts of one source as independent sources.
The retained Vivo assets are under the isolated goal integration checkout;
ASUS and restaurant assets are under the primary Translate project cache.
Both roots are explicit inputs and are read-only for this screen.

Budget: one offline capture attempt, zero model/ASR/TTS/network calls,
no retries, at most 1,920 source/target pairs, 20 seconds wall time and
no publication of private source or translated text. Preserve all inputs,
the rejected prompt results and any failed attempt. A warning can be
considered for later product admission only after checking its false
warnings and blind spots on distinct source constructions and reviewing
the exact integration/recovery contract. If the full drafts have no
source triggers, record cross-source semantic precision as unavailable.
